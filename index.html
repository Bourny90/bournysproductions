ABOUT LNAA

What is Lincs & Notts Air Ambulance?

Lincs & Notts Air Ambulance is a specialist emergency medical service providing critical care to seriously ill and injured patients across Lincolnshire and Nottinghamshire.

Its specially trained clinical teams can bring advanced medical treatment directly to patients at the scene of an incident, providing critical care when it is needed most.

The service relies on charitable support to continue its life-saving work, helping its crews respond to emergencies across the region every day.

Our support Bournys Productions is supporting LNAA by helping raise awareness of their work and encouraging visitors to consider making a donation.
